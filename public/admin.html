Bilingual Comprehension Pipeline

The Gemini prompt processor utilizes zero-shot semantic mapping to analyze Telugu vernacular complaints (e.g., "మా కాలనీ దగ్గర రోడ్డు మీద పెద్ద గోత ఉంది...") and generate standardized English summaries with recommended department triage.

Supported Output Schema:
  • Category Whitelist (Road Damage, Garbage, Water Leakage, Streetlights, Drainage, Other, Unclassified)
  • Priority Level (High, Medium, Low) based on safety hazard rating
  • Target Municipal Department (GHMC / HMWSSB / TSSPDCL)
  • Factual Civic Reasoning rationale
